# My UCPs for Portal 2 BEEMOD.
Download on the [Releases](https://github.com/maksim-terentiev/MX-BEEMOD-UCP/releases) page.

## Explosive barrel from Half-Life 2.
* Catch fire on damage and explode after short period of time.
* Leave gibs.
* Can be damaged by lasers, turrets, explosions from other barrels and anything that coded to do damage.
* Fizzling the barrel will harmlessly destroy it, either it's a fizzler or respawn.
* To place a dropper version, place the item underneath a regular cube dropper, or use the [timer to link them](https://github.com/BEEmod/BEE2-items/wiki/Default-PeTI-Items#cubes).
* Can be colored by colorizer item.
* Prepainted barrels are not supported.
